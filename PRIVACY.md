# Privacy

Ducky does not collect, send or sell any data.

- **What is stored:** the volume, limiter and bass setting for each site you adjust. It stays in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`) and is never uploaded.
- **Network:** Ducky makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, no ads and no remote code.
- **Page access:** Ducky runs on the pages you visit only to route their audio through its volume controls. It does not read page text, forms, passwords or browsing history.
- **Cross-site videos:** to boost a video served from another domain, Ducky adds a temporary header that lets the page process that video's audio. It applies only to the tab and site you are boosting, sends no cookies, never covers local network addresses, and is removed when you close the tab or change site.

Questions or concerns: open an issue in this repository.
